# Brief and story

## Ask before building (expensive to redo)
Ask these up front, in one round, with options where possible:
1. **Audience and job of the film** — who watches, where (site hero, social, sales deck, event screen), what they should
   think or do after. One single-minded message.
2. **Length and formats** — hero length (60–75 s is a good process/brand film), cut-downs (30 s, 15 s), aspect ratios
   (16:9 first; 9:16 / 1:1 later from the same build).
3. **Media** — where the footage and photos live; which are approved; anything that must not appear.
4. **Voice** — voice-over or music-only. Music-only films carry the story in titles and annotation; keep words short.
5. **Ending** — logo, tagline, CTA or nothing. Ask; don't assume a CTA.
6. **Facts** — every number and claim the film may use (years, customers, locations, specs). Anything else is `[CONFIRM]`.
7. **Brand kit** — colours, fonts, logo files (real files only), tone, words to avoid.
8. **References** — films, title sequences or motion pieces they like; ask what exactly they like about each.

## Structure (from script-structure, adapted for picture-led films)
- **Hook (0–3 s):** the most tactile real shot full-bleed, one short line. No logo first.
- **Setup (one beat):** the promise, in a few words ("It starts with …").
- **Escalating beats:** one real process step per chapter, each with a single word title, a sub line, the real media,
  one illustration of the thing, and one annotation pointing at a real detail. Vary length (4–12 s) so pace breathes.
- **Payoff:** proof at scale (a map, a number, a montage) on the music's biggest moment.
- **Close:** logo + line only. Hold ≥ 2.5 s after the last element lands, then fade.
Score the beat sheet with the script-structure rubric (hook, setup efficiency, curiosity, pacing, payoff, close).

## Beat sheet on a bar grid
Pick the music before timing anything (references/sound.md §1). At 120 BPM a bar is 2.0 s: put every chapter start on a
bar line, land titles on downbeats, and place the payoff on the strongest phrase start. Write the beat sheet as a table:
`start | length | beat | media | illustration | annotation | transition out | sound idea`.
